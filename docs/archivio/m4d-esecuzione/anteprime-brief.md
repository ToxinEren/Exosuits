# Part 4d, step (1): three art directions for the station block, previews for the owner

## Context

Redstone Exoframes, a Minecraft mod built in four variants (1.20.1-forge, 1.20.1-fabric, 1.21.1-fabric, 1.21.1-neoforge) from one tree; repo /home/user/exosuits, branch `m4-stazione` (equal to `main`). Docs are in Italian.

Read, in this order:
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-m4-stazione-design.md`: §5.1 (the station block: two halves side by side, `main` and `side`, the side half to the right of whoever places it looking at the station; a full cube per half for collision and outline; metal; **no light**; the item has a flat model with an icon), line 49 in §3 (graphics generated by scripts in the repo, committed, regenerated identically, PNGs without metadata; previews to the owner before integration), §6 (the 4d row, and "4d: (1) generatori e anteprime per il proprietario").
- `docs/arte/README.md`, the section "Direzione": the mod's art direction (heavy industrial exoskeleton, riveted plates, exposed pistons and pipes, a redstone energy source; Minecraft style, axis-aligned boxes, pixel textures without soft gradients) and the owner's own words about the frame ("più dettagliato e metallico").
- `tools/art/README.md`: the conventions of the existing art generator (deterministic output, integer arithmetic for random choices, PNG with only IHDR, IDAT, IEND, zlib level 9; numpy pinned in `tools/art/requirements.txt`), and the preview tool in `tools/art/preview/`.
- What the owner has already seen: `docs/superpowers/specs/anteprime/2026-11-19-m4/blocco-stazione.png` (a mock-up: dark steel, bronze top, copper, a red redstone screen; the main half a console, the side half drawers). Today's placeholders come from `draw_station_*` in `tools/gen_placeholders.py`; the models are `src/main/resources/assets/redstone_exoframes/models/block/station_main.json` and `station_side.json`.

## Goal

Three clearly different art directions for the maintenance station, generated by a script in the repo, shown to the owner as previews so he can pick one. **Nothing is integrated now:** the mod's assets keep today's placeholders until the owner chooses; a later plan integrates the chosen direction by running your generator into `src/main/resources/assets/redstone_exoframes/`.

## Deliverables

1. **A generator in `tools/art/`** following `tools/art/README.md`'s conventions, that writes, for a direction named on the command line, the station's assets into an output folder: the block models of the two halves in the vanilla block-model JSON format (elements are fine, but each half stays a full block or very close to it, since collision and outline are a full cube per half), their textures (16 texels per block edge: the Minecraft look), and the item icon (flat, 16x16). Use the same file names as today's placeholders where they exist (`models/block/station_main.json`, `models/block/station_side.json`, `textures/block/station_*.png`; add what a direction needs), so integration is just running the generator into the assets folder.
2. **Previews rendered from the generated files** (not drawn separately), in `docs/superpowers/specs/anteprime/2026-11-27-m4d/`:
   - per direction, the station in the world at three-quarter view from the front and from the back, both halves placed as the game places them, with the exoframe standing on top for scale (the real suit model if `tools/art/preview/` can draw it; otherwise a clear stand-in at the real size: the suit is about 3 blocks tall), lit like Minecraft block faces (top brightest, the sides darker, nearest-neighbour texels, no smoothing);
   - per direction, the icon enlarged ×8 and the textures enlarged;
   - one comparison image with the three directions side by side.
   Image labels in Italian, short. Choose how to render: the existing `tools/art/preview/` pipeline, a Python renderer, or the real client (`tools/client/client_shot.sh` can place blocks and take screenshots, but it needs the assets built into a jar; one variant is enough). Pick the cheapest one that is faithful to the game, and say why.

## The directions

Three coherent looks, each an idea you can say in a few words:
- one refines the mock-up the owner saw (console and drawers; dark steel, bronze top, copper, redstone screen), more detailed and metallic, like the suit;
- two others genuinely different in shape detail and material balance, built from the recipe's materials (`CCC` / `ITI` / `IrI`: copper on top, iron, a crafting table at the core, redstone dust) and from the suit's world (heavy industrial, riveted plates, exposed pistons and pipes, a redstone energy source).

**Anti-goals, binding (the owner's):** no recognisable Fallout, Iron Man or Halo shapes — in particular not a gantry or frame that wraps around the suit, and not the yellow-and-black industrial scheme of the Fallout power-armour station. The station is a low bench the frame stands on. The block emits no light: bright redstone texels are paint only. The owner's reference image and any asset of other projects never enter the repo; no personal data; no AI model or vendor names in files.

## Limits

- This is the first unit of part 4d and the session's budget is limited: one pass per direction, look at your own previews (the Read tool shows images), fix what is clearly wrong, stop. Say in the report what you would improve with more passes.
