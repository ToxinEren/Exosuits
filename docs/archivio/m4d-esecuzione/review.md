#### Minor

1. **The fix moved T-junctions to the station's base, where they meet the ground.** Files: `tools/art/bench.py:399` (`_conform`), `tools/art/README.md:396`, `docs/test-in-gioco/m4a1-banco.md:13`.
   - **What.** A cut must cross the whole rectangle, so the rings of cuts that make the bench conforming end on the block's outer edges. I counted these with a read-only script over both revisions' models: vertices of drawn faces (bottom faces excluded) lying strictly inside a bottom outer edge of either half (front, back and outer end, seam excluded).
     - `704cef7`: 2 (main half, front and back, at model x 15).
     - `70cbd3d`: 38 (main half: front 10, back 10, end 4; side half: 5, 5, 4).

     The top face of the ground block next to the station has only the block's corners on that edge. So each of the 38 is a T-junction across two blocks.
   - **Why it matters.** A crack along that concave edge looks into the ground under the station, where every face is culled. It would show the background (sky or fog) colour, like the pocket cracks this round removed.

     None has been seen. I ran `/home/user/exo-runs/m4d/crepe.py` myself (read-only) over this round's 49 station views: the eight shots per variant, the fourteen extra views and the three facings. Its test (`b >= 200 and g >= 160 and r >= 120 and b > r + 30`) also catches fog-coloured pixels. Results:
     - 47 shots have no hit.
     - The 3 hits are in two frames, none on the station. Two are on the far horizon in 1.20.1-fabric `dietro-vicino.png`, at y≈55. One is in open grass in `davanti-est.png` at (276,114), about 70 px from the background station; I cropped it.
     - The low view `stazione-extra/sotto-fronte.png` shows the whole front base edge at a grazing angle.

     So this is a plausible leftover, not an observed defect. The problem is that nobody is told to look for it:
     - the README paragraph "Facce senza vertici a T" now says the faces have no T-junction, without a limit;
     - the checklist asks only for dots "lungo gli spigoli degli incassi o fra le due metà".

     A dot at the base on a real GPU would be neither expected nor looked for.
   - **Fix.** Block-model quads allow no geometric fix: a ring that reaches a block edge has to end there. Fix the two documents instead.
     - README paragraph: say the guarantee holds inside the bench and along the seam, but not against neighbouring blocks, and that the base edges carry 38 such vertices.
     - Checklist note: "... fra le due metà o lungo la base, dove la stazione tocca il terreno".
     - If the PC tester reports dots there, the choice goes back to the controller.

2. **The seam faces are always drawn, though always hidden.** Files: `tools/art/bench.py:233` (the seam branch leaves `cull = None`); the rationale is at `bench.py:30` and `tools/art/README.md:370`.
   - **What.** Each half draws its seam faces with no `cullface`: 42 quads per half after the split (5 before). All 42 use `#side`, with no `cullface` and no rotation, so they come from the seam rule. That is 84 of the 995 quads drawn per station standing on the ground (148 drawn before the fix). Seam faces exist only where both halves are solid, so with both halves placed they are never visible.
   - **Why it matters.** About 8% of the mesh is built, lit and drawn for nothing, in a block whose quad count grew sevenfold in this range. The stated reason, "una metà da sola resta chiusa", does not need it. With `cullface: west`/`east` (rotated by the blockstate like the outer faces), a face is culled only when the neighbour on that side is an occluding full cube, which here means the other half. A lone half, as in the debug world, has air there and keeps the faces.
   - **Caveat.** I did not check this culling behaviour against the game's code in this review; it is the usual vanilla rule for `cullface`.
   - **Fix.** Set `cull = LOCAL[name]` in the seam branch. The pocket faces that lie on the seam plane come from the `owner` branch and keep no `cullface`, as they must. Then regenerate and run `check_art.sh`. Before relying on it, confirm `Block.shouldRenderFace` and the cullface rotation with javap on both versions, as the implementers did for the light rule; one look in game is not enough. It is cheap enough to do with the next 4d unit that touches the station models; correct the two rationale sentences at the same time.

- **`Bench.check()` does not refuse enclosed cavities** (a `cut` with `start > 0` under solid voxels). No design has one, and it would only add hidden faces.
- **`station.js` writes by default into the dated preview folder.** Git would show any rewrite of those records.
