## Context

Redstone Exoframes, a Minecraft mod built in four variants (1.20.1-forge, 1.20.1-fabric, 1.21.1-fabric, 1.21.1-neoforge) from one tree; repo /home/user/exosuits, branch `m4-stazione` at `ef73434` (main + the preview commit). Docs are in Italian.

On 27 November 2026 the owner chose direction **A, "Officina"** among the three station previews (`docs/superpowers/specs/anteprime/2026-11-27-m4d/`, made by `tools/art/station.py`; see "Stazione di manutenzione" in `tools/art/README.md` and "Stazione: tre direzioni" in `docs/arte/README.md`). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-m4-stazione-design.md` §5.1 (the block: main half where placed, side half to the right of whoever places it looking at the station; full cube per half; no light), §3 line 49 (graphics generated by scripts, committed, regenerated identically, PNGs without metadata), §6 (4d: integration after the owner's answer). ## Steps

1. **Into the assets.** Run the generator for `officina` into `src/main/resources/assets/redstone_exoframes/` (models of the two halves, textures, the item icon, whatever direction A needs). Check `blockstates/station.json` against the generator's orientation convention (the README and `bench.py` say how): in game the main half must be where the player clicked and the side half to its right, as in `a-officina-mondo.png`. The generator becomes the only writer of the station's models, textures and icon: remove the station from `tools/gen_placeholders.py` (its four textures and the item icon) and from any other writer, and delete committed station files that nothing writes any more.
2. **Checks.** `tools/check_art.sh` also regenerates the station (direction `officina`) into a temporary folder and compares it with the committed files, like the suit (JSON byte for byte, PNGs with `tools/art/compare_png.py`); `tools/art/selftest.py` gets a negative test for `Bench.check()` (a pocket open to the ground, or joined across faces, is refused). Run `bash tools/check_art.sh` → `OK art`.
3. **The real client, first on 1.21.1-neoforge.** The models were never seen in game. With `tools/client/client_shot.sh` (reuse how `tools/client/m4a1_shots.sh` places the station), take three-quarter shots of the placed station from the front and from the back, daylight, default video settings (smooth lighting on), and compare with `a-officina-mondo.png`: same faces, same orientation, no missing or stretched face, no z-fighting, no face rotated wrongly; the recessed walls may be darker than in the preview (say how much). **Stop rule:** if a face is missing, wrongly rotated, z-fighting, or the halves are swapped, do not try workarounds on the art: commit the work as it is with the message `client: la stazione A nel gioco NON passata su <varianti>: in attesa di una decisione`, and report with the shots.
4. **The four variants.** On each: `EXO_ACCEPT_EULA=true bash tools/client/m4a1_shots.sh <variant> <folder>` → its `ok` lines, `OK <variant>`, an empty `log-check.txt`; and the station shots of step 3. The reference shots in `docs/test-in-gioco/m4a1-screenshot/` that show the block (at least `*-stazione-posata.png` and `*-girata.png`; compare every one with the new runs) are redone from the 1.21.1-neoforge and 1.20.1-forge runs, without metadata. At the top of `docs/test-in-gioco/m4a1-banco.md`, after the existing notes, one note in the form of the 4c notes: with the jars after part 4d the station has its final look (A, "Officina"), the reference shots are redone, what to look for. Also confirm that no other committed reference shot shows the station.
5. **Dedicated server** on the four variants (the way earlier parts ran it: reach `Done (`, the log check prints nothing).
6. **Docs.** `README.md` line 28 and `tools/README.md` ("Texture placeholder"): `gen_placeholders.py` no longer writes the station; `tools/art/README.md`: the station in the assets is direction `officina`, regenerated and checked by `check_art.sh`; `docs/arte/README.md`, "Stazione: tre direzioni": the owner chose A on 27 November 2026, it is in the game; `docs/compat.md` only if it lists who writes the station's assets.
