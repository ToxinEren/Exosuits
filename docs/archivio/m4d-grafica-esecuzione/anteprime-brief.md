## Goal

Generators in `tools/art/`, following its conventions, that write in A's style into an output folder with the assets' structure:

1. **The screen texture**, `textures/gui/station.png`, 512x256: the 316x232 background at (0,0) and, beside it, the drawings of every widget of §5.3 in each state the design gives it (normal, hover, pressed or disabled where it has them; the frames for pieces and cell with their state borders; lock and shield; palette swatches; preset strip; cost row; the frame of the 3D preview), with the design's geometry (§5.3, the §12 layout), so that integration only replaces flat fills with sprites. Leave room the design does not use transparent.
2. **The HUD drawings**: the plate, the bar's frame and fill, the spare-cell icon, the warning and whatever else §5.12 draws, in one texture under `textures/gui/` (name it in the report). The legend colours of §5.12 carry meaning (states, energy): keep them exactly, as the design applies them. The owner's open questions 8-10 (HUD position, energy colours, vignette) keep their provisional values: draw for those.
3. **The vignette**, `textures/gui/hud_vignette.png`, with the design's corner sizes and opacity rule (at most 0,42 at the corner pixel, zero on the opposite side of the piece, two faint glass reflections in the top corners), in A's materials (the helmet's glass and the edge of its frame).
4. **The kit icon**, `textures/item/repair_kit.png` (the item `redstone_exoframes:repair_kit`, §5.8), 16x16, flat, next to the station icon in style.

Where the design or the code leaves a widget's look open, choose in A's style and list your choices in the report.

Labels in Italian, short. Compose with Python from the generated PNGs and the committed shots; no Gradle, client or server unless something cannot be judged otherwise.

## Limits

- Never touch `src/main/resources/`, Java, or the suit's and the station's generated files.
- Anti-goals, the owner's: no recognisable Fallout, Iron Man or Halo shapes in the screen or the HUD (no monochrome green terminal, no Halo-style helmet HUD). No personal data; no AI model or vendor names in files; no other projects' assets.
- One pass: look at your previews (the Read tool shows images), fix what is clearly wrong, stop. Say in the report what you would improve with more passes.
- Every new PNG has only IHDR, IDAT, IEND: check them all. At the end `bash tools/check_art.sh` → `OK art` (the existing generated files untouched).
- You do not dispatch subagents of any kind.
