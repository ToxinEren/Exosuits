## Steps

1. **Into the assets.** The generators write the screen texture (`textures/gui/station.png`), the HUD texture and `textures/gui/hud_vignette.png` into `src/main/resources/assets/redstone_exoframes/`; they become their only writers (`tools/gen_placeholders.py` stops writing the vignette and anything else they now write; delete committed files nothing writes any more). `tools/check_art.sh` regenerates them into a temporary folder and compares them with the committed files (PNGs with `tools/art/compare_png.py`), as it does for the station; `tools/art/selftest.py` gets negative cases for the GUI generators' own checks (a sprite outside its texture or overlapping another is refused, and whatever else those checks hold). `bash tools/check_art.sh` → `OK art`.
2. **The HUD in the code.** `SuitHud` draws the plate, the bar's frame and fill, the spare-cell icon, the warning and the rest from the HUD texture, at today's rectangles, with the legend colours exactly as now. The constants of the old flat look (`HudModel.PLATE_EDGE`, `BAR_EDGE`, `BAR_BACK` and the like) go or change with their tests.
